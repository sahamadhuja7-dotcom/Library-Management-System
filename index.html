Library Policy, Guidelines & Warning Notices

Mandatory operational rules, circulation conduct, and overdue / lost book penalties

How to Borrow (Issue) a Book

Check book availability in the All Books catalog. Present your valid Student ID card at circulation desk or submit through the Issue Book portal. Available stock reduces automatically.

Standard Loan: 14 Calendar Days

How to Return a Book

Return the book on or before the due date at the counter or use the 1-click Return Book action. The librarian verifies the physical copy condition and the record is closed with restock.

Instant Restock & Receipt

Renewals & Search Directory

Titles without waiting reservations can be renewed once for an additional 7 days. Use the live Search Books bar to track availability and currently holding student IDs.

1 Renewal Allowed

Borrowing Quotas & Limits

Undergraduate students can borrow a maximum of 3 books concurrently. Postgraduate students & researchers may borrow up to 5 books. Reference books are strictly for reading room use.

Max 3 to 5 Books per Student

Material Care & Preservation

No underlining, writing, highlighting, folding pages, or removing accession barcodes. Protect books from water, food, and extreme temperatures. Inspect books upon borrowing and report defects.

Zero Defacement Policy

Non-Transferable Cards

Your library access and borrowing account are strictly personal. Lending your card to another student or borrowing on someone else's credentials will result in disciplinary action.

Institutional ID Mandatory

Strict Notice on Overdue Returns & Lost Library Materials

Institutional policies require all borrowed resources to be returned timely to ensure equitable access. Penalties for overdue returns and lost books are enforced systematically.

DELAYED RETURNS

Overdue Fine & Privilege Suspension

  • Daily Overdue Fine: A fine of ₹10.00 / $1.00 per day per book is charged starting immediately after the 1-day grace period.
  • Extended Overdue (> 14 Days): Daily fine escalates to ₹25.00 / $2.50 per day. Your library account is automatically locked, suspending all borrowing privileges until dues are settled.
  • Academic Escalation: Overdue loans exceeding 30 days are reported directly to the Academic Counselor and Department Head for semester clearance stoppage.
LOST OR DAMAGED BOOKS

Replacement Charges & Degree Clearance Hold

  • Mandatory Immediate Notice: Report misplaced or lost books immediately to the librarian to freeze accruing daily overdue fines.
  • Replacement Liability: The student must provide an identical, brand-new replacement copy (same ISBN & edition) plus a ₹100 / $5.00 cataloging fee OR pay 150% of the current market retail price.
  • Clearance Withheld ("No Dues" Hold): Failure to replace a lost book blocks examination admit cards, grade transcript release, and the issuance of college transfer / degree certificates.
